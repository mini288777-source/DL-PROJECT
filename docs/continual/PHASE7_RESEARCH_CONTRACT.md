### 3. Frozen Research Questions

The research extension is bound strictly to the following 7 pre-registered research questions:

- **RQ7.1:** Does verified experience accumulation improve speaker-specific atypical speech recognition relative to the initial few-shot calibration baseline?
- **RQ7.2:** How does performance scale empirically as verified personalized sample support accumulates over time?
- **RQ7.3:** Can confidence-aware eligibility filtering effectively eliminate error propagation and confirmation bias in semi-supervised continual updates?
- **RQ7.4:** Can structured memory replay prevent catastrophic forgetting of minority emergency intent classes during continual adaptation?
- **RQ7.5:** Can acoustic and latent representation drift detection reliably identify when a personalized model requires structural recalibration?
- **RQ7.6:** Does continual quantum personalization provide a measurable performance or sample-efficiency benefit beyond continual classical personalization?
- **RQ7.7:** Does a parameterized, trainable quantum representation outperform a fixed quantum kernel under matched continual-learning conditions?

*Epistemic Invariant: No answer to any of these questions is pre-judged or assumed to be known.*

---

### 4. Formal Scientific Hypotheses

- **H1 (Continual Personalization Efficacy):** Continual verified personalization improves speaker-specific Macro-F1 and Emergency Recall relative to the initial personalized few-shot model ($N=20$).
- **H2 (Catastrophic Forgetting Mitigation):** Replay-based continual learning reduces catastrophic forgetting of early vocabulary relative to naïve incremental fine-tuning.
- **H3 (Anti-Self-Training Firewall):** Confidence-aware sample eligibility filtering yields statistically significantly higher precision than unconstrained self-training.
- **H4 (Continual Quantum Scaling):** Continual quantum personalization may improve with increasing verified experience, but this remains an open empirical question requiring rigorous matched evaluation.
- **H5 (Trainable Quantum Capacity):** A trainable quantum representation may provide representational flexibility beyond fixed QSVC, but its relative efficacy is an empirical hypothesis to be tested under strict statistical control.
