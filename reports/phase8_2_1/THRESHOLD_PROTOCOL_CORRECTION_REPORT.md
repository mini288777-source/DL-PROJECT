## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the forensic resolution of the decision-threshold anomaly identified in the Phase 8.2 Confirmation Protocol (`config/phase8_2/confirmation_protocol.yaml`), where all candidate models were listed with `frozen_threshold_source: Phase 8.1 Inner-Fold Cross-Validation (tau=0.1500)`.

A comprehensive audit of Phase 8.1 development code, threshold registries, and operating curves proved conclusively that $\tau = 0.1500$ was **NOT** an empirical model threshold. It was an accidental transcription and conflation of the canonical safety constraint ($\text{FER} \le 0.1500$, or $15.0\%$) into the model threshold specification.

To correct this defect without violating historical preservation or data quarantine rules:
1. The forensic root cause and mathematical hazards were fully documented in [`research/phase8_2_1/THRESHOLD_PROTOCOL_FORENSIC_AUDIT.md`](file:///d:/DL/research/phase8_2_1/THRESHOLD_PROTOCOL_FORENSIC_AUDIT.md).
2. A corrected, versioned protocol specification was created in [`config/phase8_2_1/confirmation_protocol_v2.yaml`](file:///d:/DL/config/phase8_2_1/confirmation_protocol_v2.yaml), explicitly decoupling model decision thresholds from FER safety constraints.
3. Candidate-specific development operating points derived strictly from Phase 8.1 5-fold cross-validation were registered.
4. Comprehensive regression tests were implemented in `tests/phase8_2_1/test_threshold_protocol_repair.py`.
5. The independent confirmation dataset inventory was re-verified, confirming that confirmatory evaluation remains **strictly blocked**.

## 2. Forensic Resolution of the Ten Required Audit Items

### 2.1 Original $\tau$ Value and Its Source
- **Value:** $\tau = 0.1500$.
- **Source:** Defined in `config/phase8_2/confirmation_protocol.yaml` (lines 11, 16, 21, 26, 31, 36) and generated by `scripts/phase8_2/generate_phase8_2_readiness_artifacts.py` (lines 205, 211, 217, 223, 229, 235).
- **Claimed Provenance:** "Phase 8.1 Inner-Fold Cross-Validation (tau=0.1500)".
- **Actual Provenance:** Textual transcription error. The value $0.1500$ was never evaluated or chosen in Phase 8.1 cross-validation.

### 2.2 FER Limit and Its Source
- **Canonical Bound:** $\text{FER} \le 0.1500$ (fraction) or $\text{FER} \le 15.0\%$ (percentage).
- **Source:** Canonical Metric Registry ([`reports/FINAL_METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md`](file:///d:/DL/reports/FINAL_METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md), Section 2.3) and Phase 8.1 Safety Recovery Protocol ([`config/phase8_1/safety_recovery_protocol.yaml`](file:///d:/DL/config/phase8_1/safety_recovery_protocol.yaml), line 49).
- **Formula:** $\text{FER} = \frac{\text{FP}}{\text{FP} + \text{TN}} = \frac{\text{FP}}{\text{Total General Speech Utterances}}$.

### 2.3 Conflation Determination
- **Verdict:** **`CONFIRMED CONFLATION DEFECT (FER_LIMIT_MISUSED_AS_DECISION_THRESHOLD)`**.
- **Evidence:** Phase 8.1 inner-validation grid search (`scripts/phase8_1/execute_phase8_1_safety_recovery.py:L125`) evaluated $\tau \in [0.40, 0.85]$. In `results/phase8_1/threshold_registry.json`, all 450 calibrated thresholds lie between $0.40$ and $0.85$ (overall mean $0.736$, comparative track mean $0.762$). The value $0.1500$ was the safety upper bound, erroneously written into the threshold field.

### 2.5 Necessity of Protocol Correction
- **Finding:** **CORRECTION WAS MANDATORY.**
- **Rationale:** Operating the classifier at $\tau = 0.1500$ on emergency probability outputs where general speech averages $0.4557$ triggers false alarms on over $90\%$ of general speech ($\text{FER} > 90\%$, over $900$ false alerts per 1,000 utterances). A confirmation run under $\tau = 0.1500$ would have collapsed clinical utility and guaranteed catastrophic failure.
